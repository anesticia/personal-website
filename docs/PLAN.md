# Product and implementation plan

## Approved direction

The site is research-first, substantial, searchable, and designed for long-term deployment on Vercel. Its primary audience is researchers, academic programs, and technically serious collaborators.

The public identity is **Andre Huizen**. The full legal name is retained for scholarly attribution, metadata, and the About page.

## Product goals

1. Establish a coherent research identity around scientific machine learning, PDEs, numerical simulation, and computer vision.
2. Present the publication record with canonical DOI and ORCID links.
3. Preserve a broad archive without representing upstream forks as original work.
4. Turn local project evidence into reviewed, safe public summaries without exposing local files.
5. Provide a maintainable contact and deployment path.

## Information architecture

- **Home:** identity, research thesis, featured work, publication, working method, contact.
- **Research:** current themes, publication record, canonical researcher profile.
- **Archive:** client-side search and kind filters over all approved records.
- **Work detail:** motivation, method, contribution, status, tools, provenance boundary.
- **About:** biography, academic path, interests, verified identity links.
- **Contact:** validated message form delivered by a server-side endpoint.

## Visual thesis

A restrained digital research notebook shaped by real simulation output: warm paper, near-black ink, precise technical annotation, and pattern imagery generated by Andre's own reaction–diffusion and wave experiments.

## Interaction thesis

- Staged hero entrance with a slow image settle.
- Scroll-triggered section reveals.
- Full-row archive transitions that make navigation obvious without card clutter.
- Fast search and filtering with live result counts.
- Motion is removed when `prefers-reduced-motion` is active.

## Delivery stages

1. Source audit and content ledger.
2. Design system and application build.
3. Typed content archive and detail routes.
4. Contact, metadata, accessibility, and responsive behavior.
5. Automated verification, visual QA, and deployment.
6. Performance optimization and security hardening with a strict no-visual-change invariant.

## Acceptance criteria

- All published identity claims have a source or explicit local evidence record.
- Original work, adaptations, and forks are distinguishable.
- The archive is searchable by title, topic, method, tool, status, and kind.
- Principal pages work at mobile and desktop widths with keyboard navigation.
- Production build, type checking, and linting pass.
- Sitemap, RSS, metadata, JSON-LD, canonical identifiers, and 404 behavior exist.
- Contact input is bounded, validated, honeypot-protected, rate-limited, and optionally Turnstile-protected.
- The global contact budget is enforced at the deployment edge, not only in function memory.
- Static imagery is content-versioned and format conversion proves decoded-pixel equality.
- Settled desktop/mobile geometry, animation timing, transitions, and CLS remain unchanged after optimization.
- No credential, student ID, raw local path, or unpublished source is shipped.
