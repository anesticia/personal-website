# Homepage prototype directions — second study

The first prototype round was rejected because it reused one homepage composition and varied mostly surface styling. This study deliberately uses ten independent page architectures. The current research content, real project imagery, live simulations, and destination routes remain the source material.

## 01 — Journal Cover

- **Visual thesis:** An oversized scientific journal cover with one live field, vertical issue furniture, and editorial restraint.
- **Content plan:** Cover statement, issue contents, one featured investigation, correspondence.
- **Interaction thesis:** Live field depth, cover-line entrance, and a narrow publisher-style progress rail.

## 02 — Research Terminal

- **Visual thesis:** A calm, credible command workspace where the portfolio behaves like a research runtime rather than a marketing homepage.
- **Content plan:** Runtime status, active jobs, evidence console, publication output, contact command.
- **Interaction thesis:** Cursor pulse, log-row focus, and a high-contrast instrument scrollbar.

## 03 — Constellation Map

- **Visual thesis:** Research projects become a spatial system of connected questions orbiting a live computational field.
- **Content plan:** Map legend, central thesis, project nodes, selected-node evidence, archive exit.
- **Interaction thesis:** Slow orbital drift, connected-node hover, and a scrollbar treated as a map coordinate.

## 04 — Academic Preprint

- **Visual thesis:** A formal web preprint with abstract, numbered sections, marginal citations, and figure plates.
- **Content plan:** Title and authorship, abstract, methods, figures, publication citation, correspondence.
- **Interaction thesis:** Section-rule reveals, figure enlargement, and an annotation-like wave scrollbar.

## 05 — Specimen Gallery

- **Visual thesis:** A contemporary science exhibition in which every project is a large, captioned specimen rather than a portfolio card.
- **Content plan:** Exhibition title, visual specimens, curatorial statement, publication room, exit.
- **Interaction thesis:** Image crop shifts, caption slides, and a gallery-scale scrollbar rail.

## 06 — Field Observatory

- **Visual thesis:** A live observatory centered on the current simulation, with sparse operational readouts and research channels.
- **Content plan:** Live field, observation readouts, active studies, method protocol, publication signal.
- **Interaction thesis:** Field perturbation, live-status pulse, and a calibrated scrollbar instrument.

## 07 — Brutalist Index

- **Visual thesis:** A high-energy black, paper, and oxide index that makes titles and provenance physically unavoidable.
- **Content plan:** Identity masthead, massive research index, publication interruption, blunt CTA.
- **Interaction thesis:** Hard row inversion, image snap-in, and an exposed oxide scrollbar channel.

## 08 — Research Timeline

- **Visual thesis:** The homepage reads as a chronological research record from publication to current SciML investigations.
- **Content plan:** Present position, 2026 work, thesis work, 2023 publication, next questions.
- **Interaction thesis:** Progress-line growth, year pinning, and a scrollbar aligned to the chronology.

## 09 — Lab Notebook

- **Visual thesis:** A working notebook with ruled paper, clipped figures, margin notes, corrections, and practical evidence.
- **Content plan:** Dated notebook cover, current question, experiment pages, observations, next action.
- **Interaction thesis:** Figure lift, handwritten annotation reveal, and a pencil-margin scrollbar.

## 10 — Cinematic Monograph

- **Visual thesis:** A minimal, image-led research monograph told through a sequence of full-screen chapters.
- **Content plan:** Opening field, three chapter statements, publication interlude, closing credit.
- **Interaction thesis:** Full-screen chapter reveals, slow image scale, and an almost invisible but responsive scrollbar.
