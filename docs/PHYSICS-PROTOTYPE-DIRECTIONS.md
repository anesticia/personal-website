# Physics Atlas prototype family

This family branches from the spatial navigation model of the Constellation Map prototype. Astronomy is removed as the organizing metaphor. Each variation instead uses a real physics or mathematics representation to organize the same research identity and project evidence.

## 01 — Phase Portrait

- **Visual thesis:** Research questions sit inside a dynamical-systems phase plane drawn in chalk, ink, and signal orange.
- **Content plan:** Central research state, three trajectory-linked projects, stability note, archive exit.
- **Interaction thesis:** Tracers follow solution curves, equilibrium points pulse, and project labels sharpen on hover.

## 02 — Vector Field

- **Visual thesis:** A directional field turns the homepage into a map of forces acting on current research.
- **Content plan:** Research vector, force legend, advected project nodes, method statement.
- **Interaction thesis:** Arrows breathe by field strength, particles advect across the plane, and nodes shift against local vectors.

## 03 — Wave Interference

- **Visual thesis:** Research themes behave as coherent sources whose wavefronts overlap to create a shared field of inquiry.
- **Content plan:** Three sources, interference thesis, amplitude legend, selected evidence.
- **Interaction thesis:** Expanding wavefronts, animated superposition lines, and bright interference intersections.

## 04 — Tensor Manifold

- **Visual thesis:** A warped coordinate manifold makes structure, metric, and local curvature the spatial language of the page.
- **Content plan:** Central metric question, curvature wells for projects, coordinate legend, research practice.
- **Interaction thesis:** Grid breathing, moving geodesic tracer, and depth changes around project masses.

## 05 — Lagrangian Mechanics

- **Visual thesis:** A double-pendulum apparatus and its action trace frame research as motion constrained by physical law.
- **Content plan:** Moving apparatus, action principle, three measured states, project record.
- **Interaction thesis:** Coupled pendulum motion, persistent trajectory ribbon, and energy readout oscillation.

## 06 — Topology Lab

- **Visual thesis:** Projects become transformations of one continuous research surface rather than isolated objects.
- **Content plan:** Morphing central surface, invariant themes, three topology-linked projects, archive.
- **Interaction thesis:** SVG surface morph, orbiting invariant markers, and elastic label transitions.

## 07 — Hamiltonian Contours

- **Visual thesis:** Nested energy shells organize work by conserved structure and changing state.
- **Content plan:** Hamiltonian thesis, three energy levels, current trajectory, method invariant.
- **Interaction thesis:** A state point travels around contours, shell intensity cycles, and labels lock to energy levels.

## 08 — Feynman Paths

- **Visual thesis:** Research is presented as interactions between physics, computation, and evidence at explicit vertices.
- **Content plan:** Incoming disciplines, interaction vertices, outgoing projects, publication channel.
- **Interaction thesis:** Particles traverse propagators, the photon line ripples, and vertices flare when paths meet.

## 09 — PDE Boundary Lab

- **Visual thesis:** A computational domain, boundary conditions, mesh, and evolving solution field form the complete homepage.
- **Content plan:** Domain statement, boundary labels, interior project probes, solver protocol.
- **Interaction thesis:** Mesh waves, boundary pulses, and heat-field contours moving through the domain.

## 10 — Fourier Synthesis

- **Visual thesis:** Rotating basis functions and a growing spectrum show complex research as a sum of interpretable modes.
- **Content plan:** Synthesis thesis, three dominant modes, generated signal, project spectrum.
- **Interaction thesis:** Epicycles rotate at distinct frequencies, the output trace advances, and spectrum bars modulate.
